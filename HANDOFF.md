## Original brief: contract-switch fix
**Problem.** The page and the accuracy logger use Yahoo's continuous futures symbols (`ES=F`, `NQ=F`, `YM=F`). The formula is implied = last cash close + (futures now − futures "at that close"). "Futures now" is the live price (`meta.regularMarketPrice`); "futures at that close" is the **daily bar** close for the close date. Around each quarterly expiry, Yahoo's intraday/live series switches to the next contract days before its daily series does, so the page subtracts the old contract's close from the new contract's price. The new contract trades higher (about +65 S&P points in Sep 2026), so the estimate runs about 0.8–1.1% too high.

**Evidence (checked 2026-11-04, Yahoo 60-day data):** `ES=F` 5-minute bars at 4:00 p.m. sat about 7 points above the S&P 500 cash close through Fri Sep 11, then about 70 points above from Mon Sep 14. The daily bars stayed on the September contract (about +2 to +7) until Fri Sep 18 and switched on Mon Sep 21. Rebuilt S&P 500 estimates (futures price at 9:20 a.m., vs 9:35 price) missed by +1.09%, +0.93%, +1.13%, +0.75% and +0.80% on Sep 15, 16, 17, 18 and 21. Normal days miss by about 0.03–0.18%. ## Methodology review (2026-11-04): findings
- **Formula kept (Matt agreed).** last close + futures point move since the close. Average signed miss ~0 (S&P -0.01%, NDX 0.00, Dow 0.00 vs 9:35); slope of actual on implied ~1.0 at 9:20; smoothing the close-day basis (5-day average, 10-day trend) did not help (S&P 0.071% -> 0.090% / 0.072%). Carry and ex-dividend effects ~0.01%/day, ignorable.
- **Futures reference is right:** Yahoo's daily close of the contract = the 4:00 p.m. price (ES median diff 0.1 pt, max 5 pts over 32 days), so after-hours earnings moves are counted (e.g. Oct 29, NQ -380 pts after 4 p.m.).
- **The official (first printed) open is stale.** Share of the futures-implied gap it captures (45 days): Dow 51%, TSX 57%, Composite 86%, S&P 87%, NDX 97%. Yahoo never revises it (its daily open = the 9:30:00 first print). The "Special Opening Quotation" (index from every stock's first trade) exists but only on quarterly expiry days, not free.
- **Best free fully formed open = index level at 9:31** (close of the 9:30 one-minute bar). 22 days (Oct 6 - Nov 4), estimate at 9:20 futures time: share captured at 9:31 vs first print: S&P 1.01 vs 0.88, NDX 1.01 vs 0.99, Dow 0.85 vs 0.50, TSX 0.82 vs 0.56. - **Other:** the 10-minute futures delay costs about 0.02 points of accuracy (S&P 0.07% with 9:30 futures vs 0.09% with 9:20). TSX proxy: ES explains about half the TSX open (R2 0.54), TSX moves ~0.87x ES. Composite moves ~0.9x NDX.
